我们处理的数据

为了提供训练功能，应用会处理训练设置，例如目标步频、时长、训练方案、音效、音量、反馈方式、主题、提醒计划和蓝牙延迟设置。您允许“附近的设备”权限后，应用还会保存本地派生的耳机标识、设备显示名称及该耳机的延迟设置。应用也会保存训练记录，例如开始和结束时间、有效与暂停时长、完成状态、目标与实际步频样本、估算步数及所用训练配置。应用不要求您提供姓名、手机号、邮箱或账号。

传感器数据

训练运行期间，应用优先读取系统步伐检测器；该传感器不可用或未获身体活动权限时，可使用加速度计估算步频。原始加速度数据仅在内存中实时计算，不写入数据库，也不包含在导出文件中。应用只保存计算后的步频样本和步数。

处理目的

这些数据仅用于播放训练节拍、显示实时步频和步数、生成总结和趋势、恢复设置、发送重复提醒，以及在您主动操作时导出或恢复数据。购买相关信息用于查询商品、处理可选的一次性支持购买及完成消费确认。

存储、网络与系统备份

训练记录和设置默认保存在 Android 应用沙箱内。训练进行期间，应用会维护一份原子写入的本地恢复快照，用于在中断后恢复训练；训练结束后会删除该快照。应用还会在不参与备份的应用存储中保留小型滚动诊断日志和最近一次未处理崩溃，用于排查问题。应用包含 Google Play Billing 引入的网络访问权限，用于查询商品、处理可选的一次性支持购买及消费确认。付款由 Google Play 处理，应用不会读取银行卡或银行账户信息；商品 ID、购买状态和购买令牌仅在内存中用于处理购买，不保存交易明细，也不发送到开发者服务器。训练与诊断数据不会发送给购买服务。应用不连接自有服务器，也未接入广告或分析 SDK；诊断数据不会自动上传。Google Play Billing 及其服务组件还可能处理购买服务的连接、错误和性能等运行信息；这些信息与应用本机生产诊断报告不同。Google Play 相关数据处理受 Google 的条款和隐私政策约束。如果设备开启 Android 系统备份，系统可能按照设备和账号设置，对应用数据库及偏好设置进行加密备份或设备间迁移；活跃训练快照不参与备份。该过程由操作系统及其备份服务管理，不由应用主动上传。

权限与系统能力

• 身体活动：使用硬件步伐检测器；拒绝后可在支持的设备上改用加速度计估算。 • 附近的设备：识别当前蓝牙音频设备，为每副耳机分别应用延迟设置。 • 通知：显示训练控制通知和计划提醒。 • 振动：提供节拍或训练结束反馈。 • 前台服务与唤醒锁：在锁屏或后台训练时维持节拍和计时。 • 开机完成：设备重启后恢复已启用的提醒计划。 网络与 Google Play Billing：查询商品并完成可选支持购买，核心训练可离线使用。

您的控制权与保留期限

您可以在“数据管理”中导出 CSV、导出或恢复 JSON 备份，也可以清除全部训练记录。“生产诊断”仅在您主动选择保存位置后导出本机技术报告；诊断日志会自动滚动，并在清除应用数据或卸载应用时删除。训练数据会保留到您主动删除、清除应用数据或卸载应用为止；系统备份副本的保留与删除由设备备份设置管理。您主动导出的文件由所选存储位置或存储服务管理，请自行妥善保管和删除。

English

Data we process

To provide workout features, the app processes settings such as target cadence, duration, plan, sound, volume, feedback, theme, reminders, and Bluetooth latency. When you allow Nearby devices, it also stores a locally derived headphone key, the device display name, and that headphone’s delay setting. It also saves workout records such as start and end time, active and paused duration, completion status, target and actual cadence samples, estimated steps, and the workout configuration used. The app does not require your name, phone number, email address, or an account.

Sensor data

While a workout is running, the app prefers the system step detector. If that sensor is unavailable or physical activity permission is not granted, it can use the accelerometer to estimate cadence. Raw acceleration values are processed in memory only. They are not written to the database or included in exports. Only calculated cadence samples and step counts are saved.

Why data is processed

Data is used to play workout ticks, display live cadence and steps, create summaries and trends, restore settings, deliver repeating reminders, and export or restore data when requested. Purchase information is used to query products, process optional one-time support purchases, and confirm consumption.

Storage, network, and system backup

Workout records and settings are stored in the Android app sandbox by default. While a workout is active, an atomic local recovery snapshot is maintained so an interrupted session can be restored; it is removed when the session ends. A small rotating diagnostic log and the most recent unhandled crash are stored in no-backup app storage for troubleshooting. The app includes network permission from Google Play Billing to query products, process optional one-time support purchases, and confirm consumption. Google Play processes payments; the app does not read payment card or bank account information. Product IDs, purchase states, and purchase tokens are handled in memory without storing transaction details or sending them to a developer server. Workout and diagnostic data are not sent to the billing service. The app has no app-operated server, advertising, or analytics SDK. Diagnostics are never uploaded automatically. Google Play Billing and its service components may also process operational information such as billing connections, errors, and performance. This is distinct from the local production diagnostic report generated by the app. Google Play data handling is governed by Google terms and privacy policy. If Android system backup is enabled, the operating system may make an encrypted backup of the app database and preferences or transfer them to another device according to device and account settings. The active-session snapshot is excluded from that backup. This is managed by the operating system and its backup service, not uploaded directly by the app.

Permissions and system capabilities

• Physical activity: use the hardware step detector; supported devices can fall back to accelerometer estimates if denied. • Nearby devices: identify the active Bluetooth audio device so each headphone can use a separate delay setting. • Notifications: show workout controls and scheduled reminders. • Vibration: provide tick or workout-completion feedback. • Foreground service and wake lock: maintain ticks and timing during lock-screen or background workouts. • Boot completed: restore enabled reminder schedules after a restart. Network access and Google Play Billing: query products and complete optional support purchases; core training works offline.

Your controls and retention

Data Management lets you export CSV, export or restore a JSON backup, and clear all workout records. Production Diagnostics lets you review and export a local technical report only when you choose a destination. Diagnostic logs rotate automatically and are deleted when app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Workout data remains until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Retention and deletion of system backup copies are controlled by the device backup settings. Files you export are managed by the storage location or provider you choose. You are responsible for securing and deleting those files.
